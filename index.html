Welcome to Losana Airlines

Losana Airlines is a virtual airline dedicated to simulating high-standard flight operations within the IVAO network. Operating out of our main hub at Mariscal Sucre International Airport (SEQM) in Quito, Ecuador, we connect South America with regional and domestic flight networks.

Rules & Regulations

  • Free Operations: Joining and flying for Losana Airlines is 100% free of charge (IVAO Rule F.4.1).
  • Mandatory Remark: All flight plans submitted on IVAO must include RMK/IVAOVA/LSA in Item 18 (IVAO Rule F.5.4.3).
  • Inactivity Policy: Pilots are required to log at least 1 flight hour per month to remain on active status (IVAO Rule F.5.3).
