Welcome to Losana Airlines

Losana Airlines is a virtual airline dedicated to high-standard flight simulation within the IVAO network. Operating out of our primary hub at Mariscal Sucre International Airport (SEQM) in Quito, Ecuador, we connect domestic, regional, and specialized air medical networks.

IVAO Rules & Compliance

  • Free Operations: Joining and flying for Losana Airlines is 100% free (IVAO Rule F.4.1).
  • Mandatory Remark: All flight plans on IVAO must include RMK/IVAOVA/LSA in Item 18 (IVAO Rule F.5.4.3).
  • Inactivity Policy: Pilots must log a minimum of 1 hour per month to remain active (IVAO Rule F.5.3).
