Rules & Regulations / Normativa IVAO

  • Free Operations: Joining and flying for Losana Airlines is 100% free of charge (F.4.1).
  • Mandatory Remark: All filed flights must include the code RMK/IVAOVA/LSA in Item 18 (Remarks) of the flight plan (F.5.4.3).
  • Inactivity Policy: Pilots must complete at least 1 flight hour per month to remain active (F.5.3).
